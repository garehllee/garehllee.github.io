CONTEXT

Lunar Gala is a fashion show attended by thousands, celebrating Lunar New Year and the arts at Carnegie Mellon.

Originally a Taiwanese Student Association event, Lunar Gala has grown to the largest fashion event in Pittsburgh. I co-creative directed the 30th anniversary show, leading a team of over 60 members across print, motion, web, photo, cinematography, and set design.

LG REFRESH

THE BRIEF

Prior to 2026's theme reveal, Creative updated and expanded the general Lunar Gala brand to reflect its origins and generate hype for the 30th Anniversary show.

FABLE BRAND

THE BRIEF

We selected FABLE as the 2026 theme to explore nostalgia and the conflict between our roots and our futures.

To celebrate 30 years of LG, it felt fitting to select a theme of thoughtful reflection. Fables are the stories and lessons that we carry, pass down, retell, and reimagine.

The core motif is a window (漏窗) that creates glimpses into other worlds. The symbol is drawn from Taiwanese and Chinese tradition to pay homage to LG's roots as a Taiwanese Student Association event.

By rearranging the window, we peer into the many pasts that shape LG today.

FABLE is a story in four parts: Follow, Wander, Trace, and Discern. The grid and colors evolve to create intrigue and set each act's tone.

The show guides us through the conflict between our past and future selves. Each line is a perspective on learning, then growing.
